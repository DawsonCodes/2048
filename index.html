THE VERY SHORT MANUAL

Good things add up.

Slide all the tiles in one direction. Two matching tiles become one, and the new value goes toward your score. Each tile merges once per move.

Move← ↑ ↓ → or WASD / swipeUndoU up to 32 moves backA little nudgeH get a hintStart overR we’ll ask first

Classic is the familiar 4×4. Daily gives everyone the same starting board and random sequence for a UTC date; restarting replays that seed. Zen opens up a 5×5 board.

Your boards, undo history, and best scores save in this browser when storage is available. Each mode keeps its own run. Hints and undo are welcome in every mode; there’s no ranked leaderboard.
